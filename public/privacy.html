Movie Tracker

Privacy & Terms

Last updated 15 November 2026

Movie Tracker is a small, free, non-commercial app run by one person for friends. It keeps the films and series you save, and the little it needs to sign you in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What is stored

  • Your account: email address, username, and the date you joined.
  • Your password: only as a salted one-way hash. The password itself is never stored and cannot be read by anyone.
  • If you use Sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link. Nothing else is requested from Google, and the app cannot see your Google password, mail, contacts or files.
  • Your lists: the titles you add, whether you watched or favourited them, the shelf you chose, your star ratings and your notes.
  • Sign-in sessions: a hashed token for each device you are signed in on, and when it expires.

How it is used

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to show your own lists on your devices, and to email you a link when you confirm your address or reset your password. Your lists, ratings and notes are private to your account; other users cannot see them.

Cookies

One essential cookie keeps you signed in. It lasts up to 30 days from your last visit and never longer than 180 days. A short-lived cookie is used while Sign in with Google is in progress. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Services the app relies on

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database, and sees your IP address as any web host does. It is also used to limit abusive traffic.
  • Brevo delivers the confirmation and password-reset emails, so it receives your email address for that purpose.
  • Google is involved only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • TMDB provides film and series information. Your searches are sent to TMDB by the app's server without your identity, and posters load from TMDB's image servers.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.

Keeping and deleting

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account; this immediately and permanently removes your account, sessions, lists, ratings and notes. To stop Google sign-in, you can also remove Movie Tracker in your Google account's third-party access settings.

Questions

Ask the person who shared the app with you, or write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Movie Tracker.

Terms of use

  • The app is provided free and as it is, with no guarantee that it will always be available or that data will never be lost.
  • Use it for keeping your own watchlist. Don't try to break it, overload it, or get into someone else's account.
  • Accounts that abuse the service may be removed.
  • This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
